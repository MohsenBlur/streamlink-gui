# Twitch Streamlink GUI

A fast, lightweight, and modern Windows desktop application for Twitch. Watch live streams ad-free with low latency, auto-download VODs, auto-play favorite channels, and stream directly through your favorite media players like **VLC**, **MPC-HC**, or **MPV**.

Everything is **bundled and ready to run out of the box**—no external dependencies, python setups, or manual streamlink installations required!

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🚀 All-In-One & Ready to Use**: Bundled with the latest **Streamlink**, **yt-dlp**, and **FFmpeg**. Download, extract, and run.
- **⭐ Auto Download & Play Manager**:
  - **Priority Live Auto-Play**: Automatically opens live streams for your favorite channels based on your custom priority order.
  - **Automated VOD Downloader**: Automatically downloads and retains recent VODs (1–5) sequentially (oldest first).
  - **Smart Filtering**: Stops downloading at previously watched VODs (> 5% watch progress) and excludes VODs exceeding your custom watch progress limit.
- **🎬 Seamless Video Playback**: Launch live streams and VODs in **VLC**, **MPC-HC**, **MPV**, or any custom video player.
- **🎨 Modern Dynamic Aesthetics**: Beautiful glassmorphism dark theme, customizable accents, and liquid layout presets.
- **🔄 Automatic Self-Updater**: One-click background updates with safety rollback protection.
- **🔔 Desktop Notifications**: Windows system toasts when your favorite streamers go live or VOD downloads complete.

## 🚀 How to Get Started

### 1. Download
Head to the **[Latest Releases Page](https://github.com/MohsenBlur/streamlink-gui/releases/latest)** and download **`streamlink-gui-windows.zip`**.

### 2. Extract & Run
1. Extract `streamlink-gui-windows.zip` to a folder of your choice (e.g. `C:\Program Files\Streamlink GUI` or your Downloads folder).
2. Double-click **`streamlink_gui.exe`** to launch the application.

### 3. Start Watching
- **Add Channels**: Type any streamer's username in the search bar and click `+` to add them to your channels list.
- **Watch Streams**: Click on a channel and hit **Launch Streamlink** to start watching live.
- **Custom Player & Settings**: Click the Settings gear icon to choose your preferred video player (VLC, MPC-HC, MPV) or adjust download paths.
